# Research

The simulator exists to do research, and the research is in the open in
[`research/`](https://github.com/dylanneve1/qsim-lab/tree/main/research). Each lab notebook states its
method, its numbers and how to reproduce them. Headline claims are checked by an independent
auditor before merge ([audit process](https://github.com/dylanneve1/qsim-lab/blob/main/research/process/audit.md)).
